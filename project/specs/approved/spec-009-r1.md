# KRR application and menu bar icons

## Expected outcome

Users can recognize Kanban Rememory Runner in Finder, in the Dock, and in the macOS menu bar.

## Scope

Replace the application icon and the menu bar text label with artwork approved by the User before implementation. Keep the menu bar item's single action: open or bring forward the main Watchers window.

## Acceptance criteria

- The approved application icon appears in Finder and the Dock and remains recognizable at small sizes.
- The approved menu bar symbol remains legible against light and dark menu bars. Clicking it opens or brings forward the Watchers window directly, without opening a menu.

## Resources

- [Proposed app icon](../resources/active/krr-app-icon-proposal.png) uses three teal Kanban columns and a white play triangle on navy.
- Proposed menu bar symbol: a monochrome three-column mark rendered as a macOS template image.
