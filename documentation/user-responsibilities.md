# Your decisions and responsibilities

You own the project and make its product and delivery decisions. In a normal project chat, one agent, the Orchestrator, clarifies the need, records your answers and carries the Card through delivery. You do not maintain the framework's files.

## Decide what should be delivered

Explain the desired outcome, priorities and constraints. Review the proposed scope and acceptance criteria with the [approval test](lean-specifications.md#the-approval-test).

Make the intended next step clear: preparing a proposal, authorizing execution and publishing are distinct. One instruction can grant several of them, and recorded standing authorization remains usable. Changes to the agreed workflow also need your approval.

## Review the result

When the Card includes checks that need you, the agent guides you through them against the exact revision and records your verdict. Before merging into `main`, it shows you the verified revision, what was checked and any automatic deployment, and waits for your explicit approval.

## Leave recordkeeping to the agent

The agent maintains Cards, evidence, the journal and authorized Git operations. You can inspect the board and request changes in conversation.

Answer in the original project chat. Optional Telegram alerts point you back there; a Telegram reply does not authorize work or record a decision.
